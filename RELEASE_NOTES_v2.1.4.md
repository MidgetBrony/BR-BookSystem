# BR-BookSystem 2.1.4

- Replaces the top-to-bottom reader's scrolling transition with a centre-spine
  page flip. Next folds the lower page upward; Previous folds the upper page
  downward.
- Adds fold shading and locks navigation until the page turn completes.
- Reduces the vertical gutter between the two visible pages from 8 pixels to
  1 pixel.
