# BR-BookSystem 2.1.2

- Repairs dark or unlit physical-book materials when asset-bundle shaders do not match the BOXROOM 1.0 runtime.
- Rebinds affected renderers to compatible runtime shaders while preserving their textures, colours, and material properties.
- Applies the compatibility repair to loaded book, comic, and magazine assets without changing saved libraries.

Validation: Release build against BOXROOM 1.0. Live in-game lighting acceptance remains pending.
